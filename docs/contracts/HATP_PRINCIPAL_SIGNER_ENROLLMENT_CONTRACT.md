## 0. Normative Language

The key words "SHALL", "SHALL NOT", "MUST", "MUST NOT", "MAY", and "SHOULD" are to be interpreted per RFC 2119 conventions used throughout this repository's other bound contracts. Every normative sentence carries a unique requirement ID, `HPSE-REQ-###`, sequential from 001, no gaps, no duplicates (§48). This contract's own numbering namespace is independent of `HATP-REQ-*`/`HBDC-REQ-*`/`HMIC-REQ-*`/`HSCE-REQ-*`, mirroring HSCE-001's own precedent of a separate namespace for a companion contract.

## 1. Purpose

`hatp_bootstrap.py` already defines `PrincipalRecord` and `SignerRecord` (Wave 2, 149O.1E) as read-only lookup targets, and `hatp_signing_ceremony.py::_resolve_signer` already *consumes* enrolled signer records for the signing-ceremony proof path. No writer has ever existed to populate either registry section — HATP-REQ-036/037 describe the enrollment procedure only in prose. Phase 149O.20L.7O.2C independently proved that three of the four unresolved `DeploymentBinding` first-use fields (`principal_id`, `signer_key_id`, `provider_profile`) converge on exactly this one missing artifact. This contract answers, in testable terms: what does `principal_id` and `signer_key_id` mean, what is the enrollment writer's exact operation/error/atomicity/audit contract, and what must change elsewhere (`hatp_bootstrap.py`, `hatp_deployment_binding_admin.py`, HMIC-001) before a real enrollment, and a real `DeploymentBinding` built on it, may exist. This v1.1 amendment additionally answers what must exist before a real *signer* — not merely a real *registry record* — can exist: Phase 149O.20L.7O.2D.1 independently proved that v1.0, built to the letter of every one of its 52 requirements, could still produce only a durable but permanently unusable `SignerRecord`. §27-§46 close that gap.

## 2. Scope and Relationship to Other Contracts

HPSE-001 governs the `principals` and `signers` sections of the existing `registry.json` document (`hatp_bootstrap.PrincipalRecord`/`SignerRecord`) exclusively. It does **not** govern:

- `AuthorityRecord`/`authorities` (the AG3/AG5 rollback-authority registry section) — untouched, unamended.
- `DeploymentBinding`/`deployment_bindings` or `authority_scope` — HBDC-001's exclusive scope (§16, §16.1, §16.2).
- The Wave-5 `hardware-credentials.json` cryptographic registry (`hatp_hardware_credentials.py`) — a separate, sibling protected artifact this contract references but does not amend. **v1.1 note:** this contract now names (§27) a required future companion contract, **HHCE-001 ("HATP Hardware Credential Enrollment Contract")**, to govern that registry's writer — HHCE-001 is not yet authored, not yet frozen, and creates no dependency this contract's own text relies on for well-formedness; it is a named future prerequisite, exactly mirroring how §21-§22 (v1.0) name a required future `DeploymentBinding` producer amendment without authoring it.
- HATP proof production/verification, human presence, or hardware attestation — HATP-001's model, unchanged.

This contract is additive to HATP-001 and HBDC-001; it amends neither, and neither requires amendment to be consumed by this contract, mirroring HSCE-001's own "additive, not amending" relationship to HATP-001/RAE-001. This v1.1 amendment does not amend HBDC-001: HBDC-001's `authority_scope` vocabulary (§16.2) and requirement-count disclosures are unaffected by this repair and are left exactly as HBDC-001 v1.2 already states them (§47, NB-5 disposition).

## 3. Terminology

- **Enrolled human approver / principal.** A human identified by a `principal_id` per HATP-REQ-014 — categorically distinct from any OS account, including the Agent OS principal and the Human/Admin OS principal (HATP-REQ-028's own two-principal topology names *OS* principals; `principal_id` names a *registry* identity layered on top, never collapsed into either).
- **Signer / signing credential.** A hardware credential identified by `signer_key_id`, enrolled under exactly one `principal_id` and one `provider_profile`.
- **Enrollment.** The act of writing a new, or revoking an existing, `PrincipalRecord`/`SignerRecord` via this contract's writer. Enrollment establishes who may approve (HATP-REQ-038); it does not itself approve anything.
- **Admin execution principal.** The Class-B Protected Administrator OS principal (HBDC-REQ-066) who runs the writer — a role, not necessarily distinct from the enrolled human principal as a physical person, but distinct as a matter of this contract's own discipline (§13).
- **Hardware credential registration.** (v1.1, new.) The act of writing a new, or revoking/deactivating an existing, `HardwareCredentialRecord` into `hardware-credentials.json` via HHCE-001's future writer (§27). Distinct from *signer enrollment* (this contract's own `enroll_signer`): registration establishes that a credential's public verification material is durably available; enrollment establishes that a `principal_id` is bound to a `signer_key_id`. §29 defines the precise ordering relationship between the two.

## 4. `principal_id` Semantics

- **HPSE-REQ-001.** `principal_id` denotes an enrolled human approver (HATP-REQ-014), restated normatively for this writer: it is never an OS account identifier, never the Agent OS principal, never a process/runtime identity, and never a bare human-readable display name.
- **HPSE-REQ-002.** `principal_id` SHALL be assigned by the Human/Admin bootstrap authority at enrollment time (HATP-REQ-037). It SHALL NOT be derived from, and SHALL NOT be required to equal, any OS-level username, UID, GECOS field, or process identity of the host it is enrolled on — this closes, as a normative rule rather than a one-time proof, the exact gap 149O.20L.7O.2C's §6 independently demonstrated by disproving `principal_id == "pcae"`.
- **HPSE-REQ-003.** `principal_id` grammar is exactly the existing schema's non-empty-string constraint (`hatp_bootstrap._require_nonempty_str`); this contract adds no new grammar. Global uniqueness within `registry.json` is required and is already mechanically enforced (`_parse_registry_document`'s duplicate-key rejection) — this contract adds no new uniqueness rule, only requires that the existing one continue to apply.
- **HPSE-REQ-004.** `principal_id` SHALL NOT change across signer-key rotation or revocation (HATP-REQ-014).
- **HPSE-REQ-005.** Principal lifecycle is exactly the existing two-value `{"active", "revoked"}` vocabulary (`hatp_bootstrap._STATUS_VALUES`); no third state is introduced.
- **HPSE-REQ-006.** Principal revocation is monotonic: the first-recorded revocation's audit evidence (§16) is authoritative; a later revocation of an already-revoked principal is an idempotent no-op that does not alter the originally recorded revocation evidence, mirroring `revoke_deployment_binding`'s "first-recorded revocation always wins" discipline exactly.

## 5. `PrincipalRecord` Schema

- **HPSE-REQ-007.** `PrincipalRecord`'s schema (`principal_id: str`, `status: str`) — already defined by `hatp_bootstrap.py`, unchanged since Wave 2 — is sufficient for this contract's v1.0 enrollment writer and is not widened by this contract. No new field (`schema_version`, `principal_type`, `display metadata`, `enrolled_at`, `enrollment_reference`, or signer linkage) is added: `registry_version` already exists at the document level (not per-record); `principal_type` has no source-supported second value under the frozen two-principal, human-approver-only topology (HATP-REQ-028); display/reference metadata is deliberately excluded to keep personally identifying data minimal (§37 of the governing prompt); `enrolled_at`/`enrollment_reference` are instead carried as audit-event metadata only (§16), exactly mirroring `AuthorityEvidence.election_reference`'s existing HBDC-REQ-065 disposition; signer linkage is the *inverse* direction already captured by `SignerRecord.principal_id` and needs no duplicate forward field.
- **HPSE-REQ-008 (disclosed schema-symmetry gap).** Unlike `SignerRecord`/`AuthorityRecord`/`DeploymentBinding`, `PrincipalRecord` today has **no `revoked_at` field at all** — `_parse_principal`'s allowed-field set is exactly `{"principal_id", "status"}`, and no `_require_revoked_at_consistency` call exists for it. A future implementation phase SHALL widen `PrincipalRecord` to add an optional `revoked_at: Optional[str] = None` field, with the identical `_require_revoked_at_consistency` discipline `SignerRecord`/`AuthorityRecord`/`DeploymentBinding` already use, before `revoke_principal` (§16) may be implemented as a production writer operation. This is a prerequisite `hatp_bootstrap.py` schema amendment — a file already bound into HMIC-001's frozen `implementation_scope_digest` set (`_FROZEN_SRC_PCAE_RELATIVE_FILES`) — and is therefore explicitly out of scope for this architecture-only phase; it SHALL be completed, and independently verified, before this contract's `revoke_principal` operation is implemented. Until then, a principal's revocation timestamp is authoritative only in the audit trail (§16), not in the registry record itself — a disclosed, intentional limitation, not an oversight. **v1.1 note:** Phase 149O.20L.7O.2D.1 independently re-verified this disclosure as real and unambiguous (NB-4); no change is made to this requirement by this amendment (§47).

## 6. `signer_key_id` Semantics

- **HPSE-REQ-009.** `signer_key_id` denotes a canonical enrolled signing-credential identity (HATP-REQ-019(d)). It is never a GitHub deploy key, an SSH host key, an arbitrary GPG key, the current PCAE agent runtime's identity, or an OS account identity — mirroring 149O.20L.7O.2C's own §8 non-equivalence proof, restated here as a standing rule rather than a one-time finding.
- **HPSE-REQ-010.** `signer_key_id` SHALL be obtained via the enrolling hardware provider's own `credential_identity()` method (or, once HPSE-REQ-059/060 land, the corrected canonical enrollment-ceremony operation those requirements name), called live during the enrollment ceremony (`HATPHardwareSigner.credential_identity()`, `hatp_providers.py`) — the identical provider-identity-exchange mechanism `hatp_signing_ceremony.py::_resolve_signer` already uses for the *verification* side, applied here to *enrollment*. `signer_key_id` is never independently invented, never human-typed, and never accepted as free-form caller input to the enrollment writer.
- **HPSE-REQ-011 (revised, v1.1 — corrects an understated v1.0 characterization; full rationale at §32).** `signer_key_id` SHALL be durably recorded at enrollment time and SHALL NOT be treated as a pure function of live device state computable on demand at arbitrary later times. **Current implementation state, disclosed precisely (not merely "MAY" occur):** as of this v1.1 amendment, neither production hardware-provider implementation can produce a `signer_key_id` at all. `hatp_fido2_provider.py::Fido2HardwareProvider.credential_identity()` and `hatp_piv_provider.py::PivHardwareProvider.credential_identity()` each **unconditionally raise** `HATPProviderUnavailableError`, independent of physical device presence, attachment, or provisioning state — this is a zero-implementation placeholder, not a re-derivability limitation on an otherwise-working method. (v1.0's text — "a conformant provider implementation MAY be unable to re-derive it from the physical device alone at a later time" — read naturally as describing a working method with a narrow later-time limitation; the actual state, independently verified by Phase 149O.20L.7O.2D.1 §5, is that no working implementation exists at any time, for either protocol.) HPSE-REQ-059 names the corrected target semantics this method must eventually satisfy; HPSE-REQ-060 names the additional hardware-provider-layer implementation prerequisite this fact requires, distinct from physical device provisioning.
- **HPSE-REQ-012.** `signer_key_id` SHALL be encoded as lowercase hexadecimal of the provider's raw credential-identity bytes — identical to `hardware-credentials.json`'s own existing `public_key_hex` encoding convention (`hatp_hardware_credentials.py::_parse_credential`). No new encoding is invented.

## 7. `SignerRecord` Schema

- **HPSE-REQ-013.** `SignerRecord`'s schema (`signer_key_id`, `principal_id`, `provider_profile`, `status`, `revoked_at`) — already defined by `hatp_bootstrap.py`, already symmetric with `DeploymentBinding`/`AuthorityRecord`'s revocation-timestamp discipline — is unchanged by this contract at v1.0.
- **HPSE-REQ-014.** `SignerRecord` SHALL carry no private-key material, PIN, or secret device state, mirroring `HardwareCredentialRecord`'s own existing discipline (`hatp_hardware_credentials.py` §"item 73") exactly.

## 8. Principal ↔ Signer Relationship

- **HPSE-REQ-015.** Cardinality is exactly one principal → zero or more signers; each `SignerRecord` names exactly one `principal_id` (already structurally guaranteed by the existing single-valued field).
- **HPSE-REQ-016.** Signer rotation (replacing a principal's credential with a new physical device) SHALL be modeled as two separate writer operations — `enroll_signer` for the new credential (new `signer_key_id`, same `principal_id`) followed by `revoke_signer` for the old one — never a single in-place field overwrite of an existing `signer_key_id` record. This deliberately departs from `rotate_deployment_binding`'s in-place-overwrite model: `signer_key_id` is itself the credential's stable identity (HPSE-REQ-009), and overwriting it in place would silently reassign what a still-referenced record's primary key denotes, which `DeploymentBinding`'s own idempotency/rotation model never has to worry about (its `repository_id` never changes under rotation).
- **HPSE-REQ-017.** A principal MAY have more than one concurrently active signer (e.g. during an overlapping rotation window); this contract does not require a single-active-signer-per-principal invariant.

## 9. `provider_profile` Semantics and Vocabulary

- **HPSE-REQ-018.** `HATP_HARDWARE_PROVIDER_V1` is affirmed as the sole `provider_profile` vocabulary value, exactly as already fixed by `hatp_providers.py::_PRODUCTION_HARDWARE_PROVIDER_PROFILES` (a closed one-member tuple). No new value is introduced by this contract.
- **HPSE-REQ-019.** `provider_profile` is fixed, compile-time contract vocabulary, not a runtime-configurable or registry-backed value. A future new protocol requires its own contract-version amendment explicitly naming it (mirrors HATP-REQ-020's own "no protocol interchangeability without proof" discipline) — never a pluggable or dynamically-registered profile list. A plugin-style provider registry is deliberately not designed here: with exactly one closed value and an existing amendment-gated extension mechanism, a runtime registry would add indirection without adding capability.
- **HPSE-REQ-020.** `SignerRecord.provider_profile` SHALL be captured from the enrolling provider's own `capabilities().provider_profile` field (`HardwareProviderCapabilities.provider_profile`, `hatp_providers.py`) at enrollment time, and validated against the closed allowlist (`_PRODUCTION_HARDWARE_PROVIDER_PROFILES`) before the write. Enrollment against a provider outside the allowlist fails closed (`UnsupportedProviderProfileError`).

## 10. Registry Architecture

- **HPSE-REQ-021.** The Principal/Signer enrollment writer operates on the identical `registry.json` document, `principals`/`signers` sections, already defined and parsed by `hatp_bootstrap.py`. No new registry file or document is introduced.
- **HPSE-REQ-022.** `principal_id` and `signer_key_id` are each globally unique within `registry.json` — already mechanically enforced by `_parse_registry_document`'s existing duplicate-key rejection; this contract adds no new uniqueness rule.
- **HPSE-REQ-023.** Every registry write SHALL preserve every other top-level section (`deployment_bindings`, `authorities`, and whichever of `principals`/`signers` is not the target of the current write) byte-for-byte unchanged except the one record being enrolled or revoked — mirroring `_registry_document_with_binding`'s existing discipline exactly.
- **HPSE-REQ-024.** List entries SHALL be sorted by their key field (`principal_id` for `principals`, `signer_key_id` for `signers`) on every write, mirroring the existing `deployment_bindings` sort-by-`repository_id` discipline, for deterministic byte-identical serialization.
- **HPSE-REQ-025.** Unknown fields and malformed documents SHALL be rejected exactly as already implemented by `_parse_principal`/`_parse_signer`/`_parse_registry_document` — no relaxation of the existing closed-schema discipline.

## 11. Enrollment Writer Contract

- **HPSE-REQ-026.** The writer SHALL expose exactly these mutating operations: `enroll_principal`, `revoke_principal`, `enroll_signer`, `revoke_signer` — plus read-only preview variants for each (§12). No `rotate_principal` operation exists (`principal_id` never rotates, HPSE-REQ-004); no single-call `rotate_signer` operation exists (HPSE-REQ-016).
- **HPSE-REQ-027.** `enroll_signer` SHALL require an existing, `active` `PrincipalRecord` for the supplied `principal_id`. Enrolling a signer against a missing or `revoked` principal fails closed. **v1.1 note:** `enroll_signer` SHALL additionally satisfy the cross-registry precondition named at HPSE-REQ-056 — this requirement's own scope (principal validity) is unchanged, but is no longer the only precondition (§29).
- **HPSE-REQ-028.** The writer SHALL be a separate, non-agent-writable admin tool — never a subcommand of the ordinary agent-reachable `pcae` CLI, mirroring HBDC-REQ-056 exactly.
- **HPSE-REQ-029.** The writer SHALL be invocable only by the admin OS principal, out of band from any PCAE-agent-invoked code path — never agent-invocable, directly or indirectly, mirroring HBDC-REQ-066 exactly.

## 12. Preview Semantics

- **HPSE-REQ-030.** Every mutating operation SHALL have a corresponding read-only preview variant (`preview_enroll_principal`, `preview_enroll_signer`, `preview_revoke_principal`, `preview_revoke_signer`) that never writes, mirroring `preview_create_deployment_binding`'s exact discipline ("never writes," `hatp_deployment_binding_admin.py:828-833`). Preview SHALL classify would-enroll / already-enrolled (idempotent) / conflict / would-revoke / already-revoked / not-found outcomes, mirroring `DeploymentBindingPreviewKind`'s existing enum shape. **v1.1 note:** `preview_enroll_signer` SHALL also classify the cross-registry precondition outcome named at HPSE-REQ-056 (i.e. "would-enroll but no matching hardware credential registered" is a distinct, named preview outcome, never silently folded into a generic conflict classification).

## 13. Atomicity and Locking

- **HPSE-REQ-031.** Registry writes SHALL reuse `repository_identity.py::_write_atomic`'s exact idiom (`mkstemp` in the same directory, `fsync`, `os.replace`, symlink rejection before and after the write race window) — identical to `hatp_deployment_binding_admin.py`'s own reuse of that idiom. No new idiom is invented.
- **HPSE-REQ-032.** Every write SHALL be read back from disk and verified byte-for-byte against the intended record before the operation reports success, mirroring `_read_back_and_verify` exactly — never reporting success on a rename alone.
- **HPSE-REQ-033.** The Principal/Signer enrollment writer and the `DeploymentBinding` writer SHALL acquire the identical, single, whole-registry-document transition lock — the existing fixed `.deployment-binding-transition.lock` path directly under the Protected Root — not a second, section-scoped lock of its own. Both writers mutate the same `registry.json` document; a second, independently-named lock would permit two concurrent writer processes to race on the same underlying file (a split-brain registry write), defeating the very TOCTOU discipline `hatp_deployment_binding_admin.py`'s own module docstring already establishes for its own writes. This requires no change to `hatp_deployment_binding_admin.py` itself — both modules simply reference the identical fixed lock-file-name constant as "the one whole-registry-document transition lock," a shared convention, not a new mechanism. **v1.1 note:** this requirement governs the `registry.json` transition lock only; HPSE-REQ-057 (§30) names the additional ordering rule required now that a second, `hardware-credentials.json`-scoped lock is also named (HPSE-REQ-054).

## 14. Error Vocabulary

- **HPSE-REQ-034.** The writer SHALL raise a distinct, closed exception hierarchy rooted at `HATPPrincipalSignerAdminError`, distinguishing at minimum: principal not found, signer not found, duplicate principal, duplicate signer, signer/principal mismatch, unsupported provider profile, revoked principal, revoked signer, malformed registry (reusing the existing `HATPTrustStoreMalformedError`, not reinvented), and read-back mismatch. No outcome in this list SHALL be represented by a bare `ValueError` or other untyped exception. **v1.1 note:** HPSE-REQ-071 (§42) extends this minimum set with four additional named members required by §27-§31's new cross-registry preconditions.

## 15. Fail-Closed Behavior

- **HPSE-REQ-035.** An absent `registry.json` SHALL be treated as EMPTY (zero enrolled principals/signers), not as an error — mirroring the existing `_load_raw_registry_document`/`HATPTrustStore._load_registry` convention (`None` return, not an exception) exactly.
- **HPSE-REQ-036.** A malformed `registry.json` SHALL be treated as INVALID and fail closed on every operation that would need to read it — never partially accepted, never silently treated as empty.
- **HPSE-REQ-037.** Enrollment against a missing or revoked principal, or a duplicate `principal_id`/`signer_key_id`, SHALL fail closed with the specific typed error named in HPSE-REQ-034 — never silently coerced into a different outcome (e.g. never silently treated as an idempotent success when the candidate record actually differs).

## 16. Audit Discipline

- **HPSE-REQ-038.** Every writer operation, including idempotent no-ops, SHALL emit exactly one `pcae.core.provenance.append_provenance_event` record against the target repository's own tree, mirroring HBDC-REQ-062 exactly. `enrolled_at`/`enrollment_reference`/`election_reference` (HPSE-REQ-007's own disposition) are carried as audit-event metadata, not as registry-record fields.
- **HPSE-REQ-039.** Audit ordering SHALL be: validate → mutate the registry atomically under the shared lock (HPSE-REQ-033) → read back and verify → emit audit record → return. An audit-emission failure occurring *after* a successful, read-back-verified write SHALL propagate uncaught rather than being silently swallowed. This is a disclosed, known limitation shared identically with `hatp_deployment_binding_admin.py` (composing two independently atomic storage systems without a real two-phase commit) — not resolved by this contract. A future reconciliation-scan tool, not built here, is the only mitigation available short of inventing a new transactional mechanism this architecture-only phase does not introduce; the alternative ordering (audit-before-state) was considered and rejected because it merely relocates the same fundamental risk to the opposite failure mode (an audit record claiming an enrollment that never durably wrote), without closing it. **v1.1 note:** HPSE-REQ-070 (§41) extends this identical ordering discipline, and identical disclosed limitation, to the now-two-registry (`registry.json` + `hardware-credentials.json`) audit picture.

## 17. Enrollment Authority

- **HPSE-REQ-040.** Three roles SHALL remain distinct and SHALL NOT be collapsed, even when the same physical person occupies all three under the frozen two-principal v1 topology (HATP-REQ-028): the human decision authority (who elects/authorizes the enrollment), the admin execution principal (who runs the writer under the Class-B Protected Administrator OS principal), and the enrolled human principal (the subject of the resulting `principal_id`).
- **HPSE-REQ-041.** No operation permits an entity to enroll or expand its own signing authority without the same fresh-election evidence required to enroll any other principal — this extends HATP-REQ-040's self-enrollment prohibition explicitly to the admin's own enrollment case; the admin enrolling themselves as `principal_id` is not exempt from HPSE-REQ-042.

## 18. Election / CHGR Requirements

- **HPSE-REQ-042.** `enroll_principal`, `enroll_signer`, `revoke_principal`, and `revoke_signer` SHALL each require explicit evidence of a fresh, separate human election authorizing that specific enrollment/revocation, exactly mirroring HBDC-REQ-064. An unverified boolean or free-form "approved" string is never sufficient authority.
- **HPSE-REQ-043.** The election-evidence reference SHALL be recorded as audit metadata only, exactly mirroring HBDC-REQ-065 — never cryptographically verified by this writer.

## 19. Hardware Credential Requirement and Provisioning Sequence

- **HPSE-REQ-044.** The first production `enroll_signer` operation SHALL require a live, hardware-backed credential satisfying `HATP_HARDWARE_PROVIDER_V1` (HATP-REQ-019/021). No software-key substitution is permitted absent a future contract version/profile explicitly naming one. This contract does not weaken HATP-REQ-021 merely to allow enrollment progress on a host currently lacking compliant hardware (149O.20L.7O.2C §7/§16 independently confirmed no FIDO2/PIV device is present on the target Dell host today).
- **HPSE-REQ-045 (revised, v1.1 — clarifying addition; original text preserved).** Credential provisioning (making a compliant physical device usable on the target host) is a distinct, prerequisite step to signer enrollment, outside this contract's own scope. `enroll_signer` fails closed (`UnsupportedProviderProfileError`/the provider's own `HATPProviderUnavailableError`) when no compliant device is present, exactly as the current FIDO2 (`hatp_fido2_provider.py::credential_identity`) and PIV (`hatp_piv_provider.py`, an unconditional placeholder) implementations already do. **This shared fail-closed symptom does not imply a shared root cause:** per HPSE-REQ-060 (§32), a compliant physical device being present and the provider implementation actually supporting HPSE-REQ-059's required credential-identity semantics are two independent prerequisites — today, as disclosed by HPSE-REQ-011, neither production provider satisfies the second one regardless of the first, so both must independently be satisfied before `enroll_signer` can succeed.

## 27. Hardware Credential Enrollment Requirement (Repair — Closes B-149O.20L.7O.2D.1-1)

`hatp_hardware_credentials.py`'s own module docstring anticipates enrollment being handled by "a future Human/Admin-only administrative surface." HPSE-001 is exactly that surface for `principals`/`signers` — but v1.0 explicitly excluded `hardware-credentials.json` from its own scope (§2) without naming who builds *its* writer. Phase 149O.20L.7O.2D.1 independently proved the consequence: a `SignerRecord` enrolled to the letter of HPSE-001 v1.0 can never produce a proof that reaches `VALID`, because `Fido2HardwareProvider.verify()` unconditionally fails whenever `HATPHardwareCredentialStore.lookup_credential()` returns `None` — which it always will absent a writer.

**Disposition chosen (of the three named in the governing prompt — (A) bring the writer into HPSE-001's own scope, (B) a new companion contract, (C) an explicit prerequisite contract referenced but not authored): (C), a named prerequisite companion contract, not authored by this phase.** Rationale: `hardware-credentials.json` is a deliberately separate, sibling *trust boundary* from `registry.json` (§2, unchanged by this amendment) — it is provider-owned cryptographic material with its own protected root, own schema, own file, and a materially different threat model (public-key material vs. identity/authorization binding) from the principal/signer identity registry HPSE-001 itself governs. Collapsing its writer's full requirement set into HPSE-001 would blur a scope boundary HPSE-001 was careful to draw in v1.0 §2, and would expand this repair phase's own mandate ("repair contract architecture only... in response to the two Blocking findings," not "author and freeze a second full contract") well beyond a narrow, targeted amendment. This mirrors HPSE-001's own existing precedent at §21-§22 (v1.0): naming a required future `DeploymentBinding` producer amendment precisely, without authoring it. A full option-(A) or option-(B) contract is not foreclosed — a future phase MAY choose to draft HHCE-001 as an HPSE-001-scoped widening instead, if the trust-boundary separation later proves not to warrant a separate document — but (C) is the minimum-scope repair sufficient to close B-149O.20L.7O.2D.1-1 today.

- **HPSE-REQ-053.** A required future companion contract, **HHCE-001 ("HATP Hardware Credential Enrollment Contract")**, SHALL govern the writer for `hardware-credentials.json`. `enroll_signer` (this contract's own operation) SHALL NOT be considered implementation-ready (§43) until HHCE-001 (a) exists as a frozen contract document, (b) has itself been independently verified, and (c) its writer has been implemented and independently verified. This requirement names required future contract text and tooling; it creates neither.
- **HPSE-REQ-054.** HHCE-001 SHALL, at minimum, define for its writer: a read-only preview variant per mutating operation (mirroring HPSE-REQ-030's own discipline); `register_credential` and `revoke_credential`/`deactivate_credential` mutating operations; schema validation against `hatp_hardware_credentials.py`'s own existing frozen `HardwareCredentialRecord` schema (`signer_key_id`, `provider_profile`, `protocol_name`, `algorithm`, `public_key`/`public_key_hex`, `status` — already defined, not widened by this amendment); provider-profile validation against the identical closed allowlist HPSE-REQ-020 already validates against; atomic persistence reusing `repository_identity.py::_write_atomic`'s idiom (mirroring HPSE-REQ-031); its own lock (HPSE-REQ-057 names the ordering rule); read-back verification (mirroring HPSE-REQ-032); audit emission (HPSE-REQ-070); and fail-closed behavior symmetric with HPSE-REQ-035/036/037. This requirement bounds HHCE-001's minimum scope without authoring its full requirement text — mirroring how §21 (v1.0) named the future `DeploymentBinding` producer amendment's required content without writing it.

## 28. Registry Separation Reaffirmed

- **HPSE-REQ-055.** `registry.json` (governed by this contract and HBDC-001) and `hardware-credentials.json` (to be governed by HHCE-001) SHALL remain two separate documents, each with its own fixed protected-root path. This amendment does not merge them: `hatp_hardware_credentials.py`'s existing schema and fixed-path discipline already establish the separation as the primary-architecture default (§5 of the governing prompt: "do not invent a parallel registry if current source already defines the schema"; "do not collapse these into one file unless primary architecture requires it" — nothing read this phase requires it). No atomic cross-file transaction exists, or is required, across the two documents; §29-§31 define the consistency and failure model that follows from that fact.

## 29. Cross-Registry Consistency Invariant (Load-Bearing)

This is the requirement that structurally closes B-149O.20L.7O.2D.1-1, not merely discloses it.

- **HPSE-REQ-056.** `enroll_signer` SHALL NOT write an `active` `SignerRecord` for a given `signer_key_id` unless, at the time of that write (checked live, under the lock ordering HPSE-REQ-057 establishes), HHCE-001's registry already contains a durable, `active` `HardwareCredentialRecord` for that exact `signer_key_id` (`HATPHardwareCredentialStore.lookup_credential(signer_key_id)` returning a non-`None`, `status == "active"` result — the identical live-lookup `Fido2HardwareProvider.verify()` already performs at verification time, reused here at enrollment time as a precondition rather than a post-hoc verification-time failure). The enrollment sequence SHALL therefore be: hardware credential registration (via HHCE-001's writer) **before** signer enrollment (via this contract's `enroll_signer`), never the reverse, and never concurrent without the lock ordering of HPSE-REQ-057. No state reachable through a conformant implementation of this contract and HHCE-001 together SHALL permit an `active` `SignerRecord` to exist whose corresponding hardware credential record is absent or inactive.

## 30. Lock Ordering Across Registries

- **HPSE-REQ-057.** HHCE-001's writer SHALL define its own fixed-path lock, scoped to the hardware-credential-store root (distinct from, and never substituting for, the existing `.deployment-binding-transition.lock` HPSE-REQ-033 names). Because `hardware-credentials.json` is a separate file (§28), its own lock does not automatically extend to `registry.json` operations, and vice versa — the two locks are independent primitives that MUST be acquired in a single, fixed, global order whenever one writer (or one logical operation, such as `enroll_signer`'s HPSE-REQ-056 precondition check) needs both: **the hardware-credential-store lock SHALL be acquired first (outer), and `.deployment-binding-transition.lock` SHALL be acquired second (inner)** — never the reverse, by any writer, in any operation. This fixed ordering prevents deadlock between concurrent operations that each need both locks. A writer needing only one lock (e.g. HHCE-001's own `register_credential`, operating without touching `registry.json`) acquires only that one.

## 31. Partial-Failure Matrix for Cross-Registry Enrollment

- **HPSE-REQ-058.** The following six outcomes SHALL each be diagnosable and fail-closed, per a conformant implementation of this contract and HHCE-001 together:
    - **(A) Credential write fails.** `registry.json` SHALL remain untouched — `enroll_signer` SHALL NOT begin its own registry-transition-lock acquisition or write attempt until HPSE-REQ-056's precondition check has already observed a durable, `active` credential record; a failed credential write therefore never reaches the signer-write step at all. Retryable without side effect.
    - **(B) Credential write succeeds, signer write fails.** The credential record remains durable and correctly registered, but zero `SignerRecord` references it. This is harmless and inert, not a security exposure: an unreferenced `HardwareCredentialRecord` grants no authority by itself (mirrors HPI-5's existing no-software-key-substitution rationale — a credential record alone is not a signer). Retrying `enroll_signer` is safe and idempotent: it re-checks HPSE-REQ-056's precondition (still satisfied) and proceeds to write the signer record.
    - **(C) Signer write succeeds, credential record missing.** SHALL NOT occur under a conformant implementation of HPSE-REQ-056's precondition check — the precondition is checked immediately before the signer write, under the lock ordering of HPSE-REQ-057, making this sequence structurally unreachable through this contract's own writer. If ever observed (e.g. through direct, out-of-band tampering with `registry.json` bypassing this contract's writer entirely), it is a reconciliation-scan finding for a future audit tool, not a runtime case `enroll_signer` itself must handle, since `enroll_signer` cannot itself produce it.
    - **(D) Audit fails after one or both durable writes.** Identical disposition to HPSE-REQ-039's existing one-registry case, extended to two: state can become durable-but-unaudited (never audited-but-not-durable, per HPSE-REQ-039's existing ordering discipline, reused by HHCE-001 per HPSE-REQ-054), for either or both of the credential-registration and signer-enrollment audit events (HPSE-REQ-070 requires them to be separately attributable). Recoverable by comparing registry/credential-store state against the audit log — never undetectable.
    - **(E) Read-back mismatch.** Each write (credential registration, then signer enrollment) is independently read-back-verified before the *next* step proceeds — HHCE-001's writer verifies its own credential write per HPSE-REQ-054's mirroring of HPSE-REQ-032; `enroll_signer` verifies its own signer write per the existing HPSE-REQ-032. A mismatch at either step aborts before any dependent write is attempted.
    - **(F) Concurrent enrollment.** Prevented by HPSE-REQ-057's fixed global lock ordering: no two writer invocations can interleave a credential-registration-then-signer-enrollment sequence non-atomically relative to each other, because any operation needing both locks acquires them in the identical fixed order, and any operation needing only one lock cannot race with an operation holding the other in a way that violates HPSE-REQ-056's invariant.

## 32. `credential_identity()` Current-State Correction (Repair — Closes B-149O.20L.7O.2D.1-2)

HPSE-REQ-011 (§6, revised above) now discloses `credential_identity()`'s actual current state precisely: an unconditional raise for both FIDO2 and PIV, independent of device presence. The two requirements below name the corrected target semantics and the additional implementation prerequisite that disclosure requires.

- **HPSE-REQ-059.** Target contract semantics (not yet implemented by any production module): a conformant hardware provider's credential-identity operation MUST obtain/derive `signer_key_id` through that provider's own canonical enrollment ceremony, using whatever protocol-specific mechanism its protocol requires (e.g., for FIDO2, a CTAP2 `makeCredential`-based credential-minting/discovery ceremony that produces a fresh, resident or non-resident `credential_id`; for PIV, the protocol's own equivalent key-generation/certificate-request ceremony) — this contract freezes the required semantic *output* (a stable, durable, protocol-appropriate credential-identity byte string, obtainable exactly once at enrollment time per HPSE-REQ-011) and deliberately does not freeze an implementation function name or call signature (per the governing prompt's own §14 instruction: "freeze semantic output requirements instead"). A future implementation MAY continue to name this operation `credential_identity()`, MAY rename it, or MAY introduce a distinct `enroll_credential()` method — any of these satisfies this requirement provided the semantic output matches.
- **HPSE-REQ-060.** Before real signer enrollment can occur for a given protocol, the selected production hardware provider implementation for that protocol (`Fido2HardwareProvider`, `PivHardwareProvider`, or a successor) SHALL implement a working operation satisfying HPSE-REQ-059's semantics. This is a distinct, additional prerequisite to physical hardware being attached to the host (HPSE-REQ-045) — the two facts are independent and both currently unsatisfied for both protocols, per HPSE-REQ-011's disclosure. `HARDWARE_PROVIDER_UNIMPLEMENTED` (HPSE-REQ-071) is the named error condition for this prerequisite's absence, distinct from `HATPProviderUnavailableError`'s existing device-absence condition.

## 33. `signer_key_id` ≡ Credential Identity (Disambiguation)

- **HPSE-REQ-061.** `signer_key_id` (HPSE-REQ-009/010/012) SHALL be exactly the hex-encoded credential-identity byte string HPSE-REQ-059's canonical enrollment ceremony produces — it is never a separately hashed, truncated, or otherwise-derived identifier computed *from* that output. This makes explicit an equivalence HPSE-REQ-009/010/012 (v1.0) already implied but never stated as a standing rule: `signer_key_id == hex(credential_identity_bytes)`, not `signer_key_id == f(credential_identity_bytes)` for any transformation `f`. This removes the ambiguity the governing prompt's §15 identifies as a required disambiguation.

## 34. Durable Signer Identity Artifact

- **HPSE-REQ-062.** After enrollment, the durable source of truth for a signer's full identity is the *pair* of artifacts, never either alone: the `SignerRecord` (`registry.json`, governed by this contract — establishes `signer_key_id` ↔ `principal_id` ↔ `provider_profile` binding and lifecycle status) and the `HardwareCredentialRecord` (`hardware-credentials.json`, governed by HHCE-001 — establishes the public-key material `provider.verify()` actually checks a signature against). `SignerRecord` alone carries no public-key material (HPSE-REQ-014) and is therefore insufficient, by itself, for signature verification; `HardwareCredentialRecord` alone carries no principal binding and is therefore insufficient, by itself, for authorization. Both are required, and HPSE-REQ-056 guarantees both exist together for any `active` signer.

## 35. Device Replacement / Rotation at the Hardware-Credential Layer

- **HPSE-REQ-063.** Device replacement (same principal, old signer revoked, new physical hardware credential) SHALL be modeled identically at the hardware-credential-registry layer to how HPSE-REQ-016 already models it at the `SignerRecord` layer: HHCE-001's writer registers a **new** `HardwareCredentialRecord` (new `signer_key_id`, per HPSE-REQ-061's equivalence) and separately revokes/deactivates the **old** one — never a single in-place overwrite of an existing credential record. Reuse of an old `signer_key_id` for a new physical device is prohibited, identically to HPSE-REQ-016's existing prohibition at the `SignerRecord` layer, for the identical reason: `signer_key_id` is a stable identity, and in-place reassignment would silently reassign what a still-referenced record's primary key denotes.

## 36. FIDO2/PIV Parity

- **HPSE-REQ-064.** HPSE-REQ-011's corrected disclosure and HPSE-REQ-059/060's prerequisites apply identically and independently to both FIDO2 and PIV — both protocols currently lack a working credential-identity implementation (§32), and both are gated by the identical HPSE-REQ-060 prerequisite before either may proceed to real enrollment. Each protocol's own future enrollment-ceremony mechanics MAY differ in provider-specific detail (HPSE-REQ-059 deliberately does not freeze an implementation), but the normative semantic-output requirement and the implementation-readiness gate (§43) are protocol-neutral. This contract does not fork into per-protocol enrollment contracts, mirroring HPSE-REQ-018/019's existing single-profile-string design: both protocols already legitimately claim the identical `provider_profile` string today (independently confirmed, Phase 149O.20L.7O.2D.1 §8), and nothing in this amendment changes that. For clarity (closing NB-3, §47): FIDO2's current design-conformance verdict is `CONFORMANT_WITH_NON_BLOCKING_LIMITATIONS` and PIV's is `NOT_CONFORMANT` (`hatp_providers.py::HardwareProviderConformance`, an existing, unmodified vocabulary) — this is a *design*-conformance fact, independent of and not superseded by HPSE-REQ-060's *implementation-readiness* gate, which both protocols currently fail identically regardless of their differing design-conformance verdicts.

## 37. Physical Credential Prerequisite Refinement

- **HPSE-REQ-065.** Four distinct states SHALL be recognized as prerequisites to a real, usable signer, never conflated: (1) a compatible hardware-provider implementation exists for the selected protocol (HPSE-REQ-060); (2) a physical hardware credential is available/attached to the host (HPSE-REQ-045's existing provisioning step); (3) that credential has been registered into `hardware-credentials.json` via HHCE-001's writer (HPSE-REQ-056's precondition target); (4) the corresponding signer has been enrolled into `registry.json` via this contract's `enroll_signer` (this contract's own existing operation). §38's readiness state machine names the ordered progression through these four states explicitly.

## 38. Enrollment Readiness State Machine

- **HPSE-REQ-066.** The following five readiness stages SHALL be the contract vocabulary for describing enrollment progress, each strictly prerequisite to the next — presence at an earlier stage never implies readiness at a later one:

    ```
    PROVIDER_UNIMPLEMENTED  →  PROVIDER_AVAILABLE  →  CREDENTIAL_PRESENT  →  CREDENTIAL_REGISTERED  →  SIGNER_ENROLLED
    ```

    `PROVIDER_UNIMPLEMENTED`: the selected protocol's hardware-provider implementation does not yet satisfy HPSE-REQ-059 (today's actual state for both FIDO2 and PIV, per HPSE-REQ-011). `PROVIDER_AVAILABLE`: HPSE-REQ-060's prerequisite is satisfied — the provider implementation works, independent of whether any physical device is currently attached. `CREDENTIAL_PRESENT`: a compliant physical device is attached to the host (HPSE-REQ-045), independent of whether it has been registered. `CREDENTIAL_REGISTERED`: HHCE-001's writer has durably registered an `active` `HardwareCredentialRecord` for that device (HPSE-REQ-056's precondition target is now satisfied). `SIGNER_ENROLLED`: this contract's `enroll_signer` has durably written an `active` `SignerRecord` referencing that registered credential (HPSE-REQ-056 itself now holds). No stage's presence SHALL be presented, logged, or interpreted as implying readiness at any later stage.

## 39. Producer/Verifier Trust Model — Documented Settled Disposition (Closes NB-1)

Phase 149O.20L.7O.2D.1 §9 independently confirmed the producer-trust/verifier-trust question the original governing prompt asked is already closed in production today, but found neither HPSE-001 nor HBDC-001 stated this explicitly. The two requirements below document the existing, already-implemented behavior — they change no production code.

- **HPSE-REQ-067.** `human_approval_trusted_provenance.py::verify_hatp_proof` (HATP-001's own verification engine, unamended by this contract) independently re-derives and cross-checks, live, on every verification call: the `signer` record for `proof.signer_key_id` (never trusting `proof.principal_id`/`proof.provider_profile` as self-asserted); `principal.status == "active"`; `authority.status == "active"`; and the resolved `DeploymentBinding`'s `principal_id`/`signer_key_id`/`provider_profile` against that same live `signer` record (not merely against the proof's own self-asserted fields). This is documented here, normatively, as the settled current architecture: a `DeploymentBinding` producer that does not cross-validate at write time (HBDC-REQ-058's existing disposition, unchanged) and an `HBDC-REQ-042` conformance check that does not re-validate authority fields at read time (HBDC-REQ-076's existing disposition, unchanged) do not constitute a security gap, because proof *verification* — the actual point at which any authority is exercised — independently closes both gaps on every call. Neither HBDC-001 nor HATP-001 requires amendment to state this: this requirement records the fact as HPSE-001's own documented rationale for why §21-§23's disclosed-but-unclosed gaps (HPSE-REQ-047/049) do not represent unmitigated risk in the interim before their own future amendments land.
- **HPSE-REQ-068.** `HBDC-REQ-042` SHALL remain scoped to deployment-identity conformance matching (`repository_id`/`canonical_deployment_root`/`status`, per `deployment_binding_matches`'s existing exact three-field scope) — it is NOT, and per HPSE-REQ-067's documented disposition need not become, the sole or primary semantic-authority validator. `verify_hatp_proof` is, and SHALL remain, the sole live semantic re-validator of principal/signer/binding/provider state at proof-consumption time. A future amendment proposing to overload `HBDC-REQ-042` with proof-validation semantics SHALL first demonstrate why HPSE-REQ-067's existing hybrid disposition is insufficient, rather than assuming `HBDC-REQ-042`'s narrower scope is itself a defect.

## 40. Revocation Model — Effective Current Disposition

- **HPSE-REQ-069.** Extending HPSE-REQ-049's existing disclosure (§23, unchanged text): because `verify_hatp_proof` independently re-checks `principal.status`/`signer.status`/`authority.status` live on every verification call (HPSE-REQ-067), an already-created `DeploymentBinding` that copied a since-revoked `principal_id`/`signer_key_id` at creation time does **not** need to be physically rewritten, rotated, or revoked merely because the referenced principal or signer was later revoked — any proof subsequently produced under that revoked identity already fails closed at the verification checkpoint, independent of the `DeploymentBinding`'s own unchanged copied fields. This is named here as the *effective current disposition*, not a new requirement on `DeploymentBinding` or HBDC-001 (which this contract does not govern, §2) — it is a documentation-only clarification of behavior Phase 149O.20L.7O.2D.1 §9 independently confirmed already holds in production. A future HBDC-001 amendment MAY still choose to add an explicit revocation-cascade check to `HBDC-REQ-042` for defense-in-depth or auditability reasons, but HPSE-REQ-067/069 together establish that no such amendment is required to close a live security gap — none exists.

## 41. Audit Ordering Across Registries

- **HPSE-REQ-070.** Hardware credential registration (HHCE-001's `register_credential`/`revoke_credential`) and signer enrollment (this contract's `enroll_signer`/`revoke_signer`) SHALL each emit their own separately attributable audit event, per HPSE-REQ-038's existing one-event-per-operation discipline (reused, not altered, by HHCE-001 per HPSE-REQ-054). An audit trail SHALL NOT record or imply that signer enrollment occurred if only hardware credential registration occurred, and vice versa — the two operations are audited as the two distinct acts they are, even when performed as part of one logical administrative session enrolling one new signer end-to-end (HPSE-REQ-058(B)/(D)'s partial-failure cases depend on this distinction being real and mechanically enforced, not merely conventional).

## 42. Error Vocabulary Additions
